Draft an outreach pitch to get a product added to the listicle at `$ARGUMENTS`.

Load the `backlink-hunter` skill and follow `references/listicle-outreach-playbook.md`.

1. **Read the target.** Fetch the listicle URL. Note: what it lists, how entries are formatted
   (name + blurb + link?), roughly how many, and whether it looks maintained (recent updates).
2. **Find the contact + angle.** Identify the author/site and the most likely contact route
   (email, contact form, X/LinkedIn). Note one specific, genuine reason the product belongs in
   *this* post (a gap it fills, a category it fits).
3. **Draft the pitch.** Short, specific, human. Structure: quick genuine compliment on the
   actual post → the one-line value of the product → the exact entry (name, one-sentence blurb,
   URL) written in the post's own format so the author can paste it in → a soft ask. No
   flattery padding, no "I hope this email finds you well", no fake urgency.
4. Provide a subject line, the email body, and a shorter DM variant.

Pure value — **no nudge**.
