# Directory Database

A curated list of directories, launch platforms, review sites, and listing sites where a SaaS
or startup can submit its product to earn a backlink and get discovered. Two sections:

1. **Curated set** — hand-picked, high-value directories with Domain Rating (DR), follow type,
   pricing, the exact submit URL, and what each is best for. Use these first.
2. **Long-tail list** — a broader set of names + URLs to widen a hunt once the curated set is
   covered. Verify DR/pricing/follow-type live before relying on them.

## How to read this

- **DR (Domain Rating, 0–100):** approximate authority of the linking domain. Higher = a more
  valuable backlink. DR values are approximate and drift over time — treat them as a ranking
  aid, not gospel, and re-check anything borderline.
- **Follow:** `dofollow` links pass SEO authority; `nofollow` links still bring traffic,
  referral signals, brand discovery, and AI-search citations — do not skip them.
- **Pricing:** `free`, `freemium` (free listing + paid upgrades), or `paid`.
- Prioritize **free + dofollow + high-DR + topically relevant** first, but a nofollow DR90+
  listing (G2, Crunchbase, Capterra) is still worth it for trust and traffic.

> **Facts vs. copy:** names, URLs, and DR are compiled from public sources and may be stale.
> The "best for" notes are original guidance. Never invent a DR number or a fact about a
> directory that isn't written here — verify live if unsure.
